# Scoring

Versioned rubrics assess context, structure, clarity, directness, precision, listener load and delivery. Four criteria per dimension, each 0 (material gap), 1 (partial/late) or 2 (explicit/sufficient), with evidence and explanation. Scores use coarse 5-point rounding of the 0–100 criterion sum. Unassessable criteria produce unavailable dimensions, not zero. No overall score lets fluency dominate clarity. Higher listener-load score means easier to follow.

Only comparable rubric/provider profiles produce before/after deltas. Hinglish, accent, grammar and thinking pauses are not faults absent listener consequence. ASR uncertainty and chunk stitching are separate from likely speaker issues. Normally at most three actionable distinct supported patterns are shown.
